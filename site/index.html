    Ukrainian science fiction, 1920–2020

    Research by Oleh Shynkarenko, a Ukrainian writer and PhD candidate at the University of Pécs, on how a century of Ukrainian science fiction moved from the techno-communist utopias of the 1920s to the irony and doubt of the post-Soviet decades. Here you will find author profiles, peer-reviewed articles, essays and his own fiction.

    About

    Oleh Shynkarenko

    PhD candidate, Doctoral School of Literary and Cultural Studies, University of Pécs

    Born in Zaporizhzhia, I trained as an automation engineer, spent more than twenty years in journalism and have published six books of fiction, one of them in English translation. My research reads Ukrainian science fiction as a record of how an era believed in technology — and how that belief wore out.
