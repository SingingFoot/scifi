Motivation

Why Ukrainian science fiction

I grew up in a society where philosophy was largely off limits, and Ukrainian science fiction was where I found it. Writers such as Volodymyr Savchenko taught me to ask philosophical questions through stories.

I read it now as a record of Ukrainian intellectual history, and as part of world science fiction rather than a provincial branch of it.
