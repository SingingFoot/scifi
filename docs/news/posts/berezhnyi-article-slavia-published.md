The article compares Berezhnyi's early utopian novel <em>To the Starry Worlds</em> (1956) with his last collection <em>Labyrinth</em> (1986). It traces his path from the state-sanctioned optimism of the early space age to postmodern irony and reads it as a mirror of the exhaustion of the Soviet techno-communist project. The full text is available on this site: <a href="/phd/berezhnyi-techno-communism/">Vasyl Berezhnyi: Collapse of Techno-Communism</a>.
