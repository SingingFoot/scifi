# Ostrikov Article Accepted by Galaxies

Oleh Shynkarenko's article on Pavlo Ostrikov's science-fiction film has been accepted for publication, in French translation, by [Galaxies](https://galaxiessf.com/), the French science-fiction magazine. It is set to appear in issue 96, alongside a short story by Veronika Volynska.

<!-- more -->

Since the start of Russia's full-scale invasion, Galaxies has featured a piece of Ukrainian science fiction in every issue, often paired with critical essays introducing the genre's history to French readers.
