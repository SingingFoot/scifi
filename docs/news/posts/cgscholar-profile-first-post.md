Oleh Shynkarenko has opened a <a href="https://cgscholar.com/user-profile/211765">profile on CGScholar</a>, the academic social network of the Common Ground Research Networks, and published his first post there, <a href="https://cgscholar.com/posts/7156">"Does AI Understand?"</a>.
</p>

   data-ua="Допис повертається до «Китайської кімнати» Джона Серла в добу великих мовних моделей і спирається на препринт &lt;a href=&quot;https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22639192&quot;&gt;&lt;i&gt;AI: The Illusion of Understanding&lt;/i&gt;&lt;/a&gt;. The post returns to John Searle's Chinese Room in the age of large language models and draws on the preprint <a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22639192"><i>AI: The Illusion of Understanding</i></a>. It asks how teachers and researchers can tell genuine understanding from skilled imitation, a question that predates artificial intelligence but has become impossible to ignore.
</p>
