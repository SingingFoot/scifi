# Zayets Article Accepted at the Journal of Science Fiction and Philosophy

Oleh Shynkarenko's article on Volodymyr Zayets has been accepted for publication in the [Journal of Science Fiction and Philosophy](https://jsfphil.org/), following peer review. The piece examines Zayets' fiction in light of postmodernism and the political climate of his time. A revised version is now in preparation ahead of copyediting.

<!-- more -->

Reviewers praised the article's engagement with science fiction and its philosophical scope, and recommended only minor revisions before publication. The final text will be announced here once it is available.
