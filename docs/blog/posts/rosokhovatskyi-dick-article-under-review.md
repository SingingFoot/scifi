# Rosokhovatskyi and Dick Article Now Under Review at Science Fiction Studies

Oleh Shynkarenko's revised article, "The Syhom and the Android: Ihor Rosokhovatskyi, Philip K. Dick, and the Cold War Argument about Machine Ethics," has been resubmitted and is now under review at [Science Fiction Studies](https://online.ucpress.edu/sfs), widely regarded as the field's premier academic journal.

<!-- more -->

The article reads Rosokhovatskyi's synthetic "syhom" beings alongside Dick's androids to recover a Cold War argument about machine ethics that Anglophone criticism has so far only heard one side of. Reviewers have now been invited to evaluate the manuscript.
