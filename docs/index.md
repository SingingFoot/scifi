# Ukrainian science fiction, 1920–2020

Research by Oleh Shynkarenko, a Ukrainian writer and PhD candidate at the
University of Pécs (Doctoral School of Literary and Cultural Studies), on how
Ukrainian science fiction moved from the techno-communist utopias of the 1920s
to the irony and doubt of the post-Soviet decades.
